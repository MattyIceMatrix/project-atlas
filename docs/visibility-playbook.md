# The Visibility Playbook: Getting in Front of the People Who Lead AI and Quantum

```text
        LEVEL 5   INVITED      mentors, labs and institutes come to you
           ^
        LEVEL 4   SELECTED     fellowships: MATS, Anthropic Fellows
           ^
        LEVEL 3   PUBLISHED    workshop paper, Alignment Forum post, upstream eval or decoder
           ^
        LEVEL 2   COMPETED     Apart sprints, QHack, iQuHACK, unitaryHACK
           ^
        LEVEL 1   CONTRIBUTED  merged PRs to tools the leaders themselves use
```

Leaders in AI and quantum don't discover people through resumes. They meet them through **work they
can inspect**: a merged pull request to a tool their team uses, a hackathon project their researchers
judged, a paper at a workshop they organise, a fellowship they mentor in. Every frontier project in
the atlas (101–121) is aimed at one or more of these doors.

> Dates and terms change every cycle. Check each program's official page before planning around it.
> Status below was checked in November 2026.

## AI safety and alignment doors


**Apart Research sprints** are open-to-all weekend research hackathons; no team, prior sprint
experience or AI safety background is required. Topics are often co-designed with leading safety
organisations; the AI Control hackathon, for example, is co-organised with Redwood Research. There are
local hubs in Germany: AI Safety Saarland hosts an in-person hub in Saarbrücken, and Berlin hubs run
too. *Start here: it's the fastest way to have your work judged by professionals.*

**Inspect Evals** is the open-source evaluation library built on the UK AI Security Institute's Inspect
framework, used by UK AISI, METR, RAND and others for safety testing, and it accepts community
contributions. A merged eval is work those organisations can run themselves.

**MATS** is a mentored research fellowship connecting fellows with mentors from groups such as
Anthropic's Alignment Science team, UK AISI, Redwood Research and ARC. It now runs several cohorts a
year, with tracks including technical governance and systems security. Those two match your
security background (projects 106, 110).

**Anthropic Fellows Program** provides funding and mentorship for engineers and researchers to work
on safety research areas including scalable oversight, adversarial robustness and AI control, model
organisms, mechanistic interpretability, AI security and model welfare. Applications are accepted on a
rolling basis, and the original program was aimed at mid-career technical professionals moving into
safety research, regardless of background.

## Quantum doors

```text
   DOOR                         WHAT IT IS                                  ATLAS PROJECTS
   QHack (Xanadu/PennyLane)     annual coding championship, open entry      112 113 115 119
   iQuHACK (MIT)                annual quantum hackathon                    113 118
   unitaryHACK                  annual open-source bounty hackathon         111 114 116
   Unitary Foundation grants    rolling microgrants for open quantum work   111 114 116
   Deltakit Community Fund      QEC microgrants with Riverlane mentorship   111
```

**QHack** is Xanadu's annual quantum coding competition built on PennyLane, open to everyone from
first-time quantum coders to researchers, with no selection to enter. The 2026 edition ran in October.

**Unitary Foundation** runs a microgrant program (about $4,000) for open quantum projects, and
**unitaryHACK** each June pays bounties for closing issues in open-source quantum projects. With
Riverlane, it also launched the **Deltakit Community Fund**: quarterly $2,000–4,000 microgrants plus
mentorship from Riverlane and Unitary engineers for quantum error correction features. That's the exact
target for QDECODER (111).

## The 12-month visibility plan (alongside LANTERN)

```text
  MONTH  1-2   one merged PR to Inspect Evals or Mitiq          (level 1: contributed)
  MONTH  3     Apart Research sprint, Saarbrücken or online      (level 2: competed)
  MONTH  4-6   EVALSMITH or GLASSBOX: one public write-up        (level 3: published)
  MONTH  6     apply: Anthropic Fellows (rolling) and next MATS  (level 4: selected)
  MONTH  7-9   unitaryHACK bounties + Unitary/Deltakit microgrant (quantum track, level 1-2)
  MONTH 10     QHack coding challenges                           (level 2)
  MONTH 11-12  workshop paper submission + NATIONSTACK white paper (level 3-5)
```

## Rules that make this work

1. **Ship small and public first.** A merged PR beats a private masterpiece nobody can see.
2. **Choose problems leaders have publicly asked for.** Labs publish research directions; build those.
3. **Your edge is your background.** A combat-arms veteran with security training, building disaster AI
   in Germany, is a story no one else has. Technical governance, AI control and crisis resilience fit it.
4. **Honest results travel further than hype.** Negative results in quantum ML or failed controls in AI
   safety are publishable and respected.
5. **Follow up like a professional.** After a sprint or a merged PR, thank the reviewers, share the next result.
